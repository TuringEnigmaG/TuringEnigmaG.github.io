两种语言，同一个身体Two languages, one body

两千年前，中医面对的是同样的人，却用完全不同的语言记录了人体的规律。这不是两种医学的对立，而是两种观察世界的方式。

Two thousand years ago, Chinese medicine faced the same human body but described it in a completely different vocabulary. These aren't competing systems. They're two ways of observing the same reality.

现代医学 · 看结构Modern medicine · structure细胞、器官、分子、炎症因子Cells, organs, molecules, inflammatory markers

关心已经发生的问题：血糖高，诊断糖尿病。

Focuses on what has already happened: high blood sugar, diagnose diabetes.

中医 · 看状态Chinese medicine · state气血是否流畅、燥湿是否平衡、有没有“热”Is qi and blood flowing? Is dryness and dampness balanced? Is there “heat”?

关心正在发展的变化：血糖还正常时，已能察觉口干、易饿、心烦的趋势。

Focuses on where things are heading: thirst, hunger and restlessness can show the trend before blood sugar rises.

    结语Closing

    中医不是过时，是因为它描述的是人体永恒的规律——循环、免疫、代谢、情绪、睡眠、气血。而现代医学提供了看清这些规律的显微镜。

    中医不是神秘，而是另一套逻辑。现代医学不是否定中医，而是照亮中医。

    Chinese medicine isn't outdated, because it describes the body's lasting patterns: circulation, immunity, metabolism, mood, sleep, qi and blood. Modern medicine provides the microscope to see them clearly.

    Chinese medicine isn't mysticism; it's another logic. Modern medicine doesn't refute it; it illuminates it.
